Digital Book

Mission books that feel like a real text

Each mission unlocks a digital book: cover, page-turn spreads, figures, and glossary terms. Books reinforce what you just played. Finish the 10 steps to unlock, or use Unlock books on the landing page for demos.

What it is

A full-screen book viewer built into every game. Fixed pages (no endless scroll), optional figure carousels, and clickable glossary words.

  • Cover + Open / Prev / Next
  • Illustrated spreads from curriculum books
  • Red glossary terms → tutor
  • Per-mission book packs in books/

How a student uses it

  • Complete a mission (or unlock books)
  • On the hub, tap Read book
  • Tap the cover or Open
  • Turn pages through theory spreads
  • Tap a red word to ask the tutor
  • Close and return to missions

What you see on screen

  • Cover: title, hook line, art
  • Spreads: text + figures
  • Controls: Prev / status / Open or Next
  • Close: dismiss overlay
  • Terms: highlighted vocabulary

Locking + unlock

  • Default: locked until mission done
  • Landing toggle: Unlock books: On
  • Stored in browser (demo-friendly)
  • Same book engine for all 28 games

Example in the snapshot

Living or Not opens on a navy cover: “seven signs of life, then the cases that fool a gut check.” The book walks MRS GREN and tricky cases after the Canvas mission, so play and reading stay linked. Engine files: engine/js/digital-book.js, engine/css/digital-book.css, engine/js/mission-books.js.
